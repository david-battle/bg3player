# Baldur's Gate 3 - Classes and Subclasses

The 12 classes and every subclass. Each class entry gives its role, hit points, key abilities, spellcasting ability (where relevant) and starting proficiencies, then the subclasses with a one-line summary of what sets each apart. Multiclass proficiencies are what a character gains for dipping into the class. There are no full level tables here; that detail lives on bg3.wiki's per-class pages.

## Barbarian

Barbarians use their martial prowess and primal rage to strengthen themselves and dominate enemies in combat.

- Hit points: At level 1: 12 + Constitution modifier On level up: 7 + Constitution modifier | Key abilities: Strength for improved attack rolls and damage with melee weapons. Dexterity for higher initiative and Armour Class Constitution for more hit points and unarmoured Armour Class. | Spellcasting ability: Charisma for the effective use of actions, items, scrolls, and equipment.
- Saving throw proficiencies: Strength, Constitution
- Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons, Martial weapons Light armour, Medium armour Shields
- Skill proficiencies (Choose 2): Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Survival, Perception
- Starting Equipment: Greataxe, Handaxe x2, Barbarian Clothes, Leather Boots
- Multiclass Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons, Martial weapons Shields

Subclasses:
- **Berserker** - These Barbarians are more specialized in using their anger to gain more abilities while raging in combat.
- **Wildheart** - These Barbarians are attuned with nature and the beasts that live in it. Their rages resemble the animals that inspired them.
- **Wild Magic (barbarian subclass)** - These barbarians manifest otherworldy magic to bolster their martial abilities.
- **Giant (barbarian subclass)** - They can grow in size while raging and kick and throw other creatures. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Bard

Bards are expert storytellers, artists, and performers. Bards channel their power through song, speech, or performance to cast Spells . Bards use Charisma as their primary ability score.

- Hit points: At level 1: 8 + Constitution modifier On level up: 5 + Constitution modifier | Spellcasting ability: Charisma
- Saving Throw proficiencies: Dexterity, Charisma
- Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons, Hand Crossbows, Rapiers, Longswords, Shortswords Light armour
- Skills with proficiency (Choose 3): History, Insight, Medicine, Persuasion, Religion, Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth, Animal Handling, Arcana, Investigation, Athletics, Nature, Perception, Survival You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
- Starting Equipment: Hand Crossbow, Simple Jerkin, Leather Boots, an Instrument chosen during character creation
- Multiclass Equipment proficiencies: Light armour
- Multiclass Skills with proficiency (Choose 1): Same list as Starting Proficiencies
- Multiclass Granted equipment: An instrument chosen when taking the first Bard level

Subclasses:
- **College of Lore** - These Bards gain the ability to hinder their foes with their performances, 3 more Skill Proficiencies, and additional pick of Magical Secrets spells.
- **College of Valour** - These Bards alter Bardic Inspiration so that it increases the combat prowess of their allies, and receive a moderate increase to their own combat prowess by obtaining all weapon proficiencies, all but heavy armour, and an extra attack.
- **College of Swords** - These Bards focus entirely on improving their own combat prowess by combining the Extra Attack passive, being able to use Bardic Inspiration charges for utility and multi-attacks, and being the only Bard Subclass able to have a Fighting Style.
- **College of Glamour** - It can inspire multiple allies simultaneously with temporary hit points and influence enemies. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.
- **Bladesinging** - It uses an elegant combination of sword and spell to defeat enemies. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Cleric

Clerics worship and enact their deity's will to the world, for better or worse. They channel the power of their faith to cast Spells . Clerics use Wisdom as their primary ability score.

- Hit points: At level 1: 8 + Constitution modifier On level up: 5 + Constitution modifier | Key abilities: Wisdom for Spells Constitution for Hit Points and Concentration checks Dexterity for Armour Class.
- Saving throw proficiencies: Wisdom, Charisma
- Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons, Flails, Morningstars Light armour, Medium armour Shields
- Skill proficiencies (Choose 2): History, Insight, Medicine, Persuasion, Religion You receive a bonus to every skill that you are proficient in. At character level 1, this proficiency bonus is +2.
- Starting equipment: Mace, Chain Shirt, Studded Shield, Leather Boots
- Multiclass Equipment proficiencies: Flails, Morningstars Light armour, Medium armour Shields
- Multiclass Skill proficiencies (Choose 2[1]): Same list as Starting Proficiencies

Subclasses:
- **Life Domain** - These Clerics are more specialized in preserving life, and their healing spells are more potent.
- **Light Domain** - These Clerics are charged with chasing away lies and burning away darkness.
- **Trickery Domain** - These Clerics are more specialised in stealth and illusion magic.
- **Knowledge Domain** - These Clerics are more specialized in arcane magic.
- **Nature Domain** - The player-selectable deities directly associated with this domain are Eilistraee and Mielikki.
- **Tempest Domain** - Tempest Domain is one of the cleric subclasses, with a focus on Thunder and Lightning damage, weather themed spells, and martial combat.
- **War Domain** - These Clerics are more specialized in striking with weapons, dealing physical damage.
- **Death Domain** - It has potent features which boost necrotic spells and add necrotic damage to weapon attacks. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Druid

Druids are closely attuned with nature and the animals that live in it. They utilize the power of nature to cast Spells and have the ability to transform into various creatures. Druids use Wisdom as their primary ability score.

- Hit points: At level 1: 8 + Constitution modifier On level up: 5 + Constitution modifier
- Saving throw proficiencies: Intelligence, Wisdom
- Equipment proficiencies: Clubs, Daggers, Javelins, Maces Quarterstaves, Scimitars, Sickles, Spears Light armour, Medium armour Shields
- Skills with proficiency (choose 2): Arcana, Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Religion, Survival You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
- Starting Equipment: Druid Quarterstaff, Druid Leather Armour, Grovetender Boots
- Multiclass Equipment proficiencies: Light armour, Medium armour Shields

Subclasses:
- **Circle of the Land** - These Druids are more specialized in spellcasting to attack enemies or support allies in combat. They also have the ability to recover a certain amount of Spell Slots once per day.
- **Circle of the Moon** - These Druids are more specialized in Wild Shaping into various creatures to attack enemies or support allies in combat. They also have the ability to regain Hit Points while Wild Shaped.
- **Circle of the Spores** - These druids use fungal growth and decay to inflict Necrotic damage through spells, as well as to raise corpses through fungal infestation to fight at their side.
- **Circle of the Stars** - It can choose to transform into special Starry Forms instead of beast forms which grant unique abilities. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Fighter

Fighters use their martial expertise to fight enemies and support their allies in combat. Fighters use Strength or Dexterity as their primary ability score.

- Hit points: At Level 1: 10 + Constitution Modifier On level up: 6 + Constitution Modifier | Spellcasting ability: Intelligence for the effective use of items, scrolls, and equipment.
- Saving throw proficiencies: Strength, Constitution
- Equipment Proficiencies: Simple weapons, Martial weapons Light armour, Medium armour, Heavy armour Shields
- Skills proficiencies (Choose 2): Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Perception, Survival You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
- Starting Equipment: Longsword, Scale Mail, Leather Boots
- Multiclass Equipment Proficiencies: Simple weapons, Martial weapons Light armour, Medium armour Shields

Subclasses:
- **Battle Master** - They focus on using active skills on a limited resource to hinder and disable opponents as well as sometimes supporting allies.
- **Eldritch Knight** - These Fighters specialize in spellcasting, using their martial prowess and magic to attack enemies or support allies in combat. Eldritch Knights use Intelligence as their Spellcasting Ability, and learn spells from the Wizard spell list, favouring the schools of Evocation and Abjuration.
- **Champion** - This subclass obtains all the features from its base class, Fighter, in addition to its unique features outlined below.
- **Arcane Archer** - It focuses on using magical arrows to damage and control enemies. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Monk

Monks are unarmed combatants capable of spending Ki Points to perform special abilities. Monk's key ability scores are Wisdom , Dexterity , and Constitution , and their core ability is Unarmed Strike .

- Hit points: At level 1: 8 + Constitution modifier On level up: 5 + Constitution modifier
- Saving throw proficiencies: Strength, Dexterity
- Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons, Shortswords
- Skills with proficiency (Choose 2): History, Insight, Religion, Acrobatics, Stealth, Athletics You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
- Starting Equipment: Quarterstaff, Monastic Robes, Monastic Boots
- Multiclass Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons, Shortswords

Subclasses:
- **Way of the Open Hand** - It can be chosen at level 3. These monks are more specialized in using unarmed attacks to strike their opponents down.
- **Way of Shadow** - It can be chosen at level 3. These monks are more specialized in using the shadows to quietly strike their foes.
- **Way of the Four Elements** - These monks are more specialized in using their Ki to channel the elements to influence the battlefield. Rather than focusing on physical techniques that the other Monk subclasses focus on, Way of the Four Elements focuses on abilities that mimic Wizard spells, turning monks into a hybrid spellcaster.
- **Way of the Drunken Master** - They have significant bonuses against drunk targets and can intoxicate enemies in combat. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Paladin

Paladins are masters of a variety of weapons and armour. They channel the power of their sacred oath to heal the sick and injured and to smite their foes. Important Ability Scores for Paladins include Strength (or Dexterity ), Charisma , and Constitution .

- Hit points: At level 1: 10 + Constitution modifier On level up: 6 + Constitution modifier | Key abilities: Strength or Dexterity for physical attacks Charisma for Spells, Aura and Channel Oath Abilities Constitution for Hit Points and Concentration Checks. | Spellcasting ability: Charisma
- Saving throw proficiencies: Wisdom, Charisma
- Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons, Martial weapons Light armour, Medium armour, Heavy armour Shields
- Skills with proficiency (Choose 2): Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Persuasion, Religion You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
- Starting Equipment: Warhammer, Wooden Shield, Javelin x2, Scale Mail of the Ancients or Scale Mail of Devotion or Scale Mail of Vengeance or Scale Mail of the Crown, Leather Boots and Paladin Oaths and their Tenets
- Multiclass Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons, Martial weapons Light armour, Medium armour Shields

Subclasses:
- **Oath of Devotion** - These Paladins gain abilities that aid them in protecting the weak, dispelling falsehoods, and defeating fiends and undead.
- **Oath of the Ancients** - These Paladins have a deep connection with the natural world and the light within it.
- **Oath of Vengeance** - Oath of Vengeance is one of the subclasses of Paladin that focuses on righting wrongs and delivering justice, usually by any means necessary.
- **Oath of the Crown** - It focuses on protecting allies by healing them and redirecting attacks. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.
- **Oathbreaker** - These Paladins draw power from the darkness within to destroy their foes.

## Ranger

In tune with nature and their surroundings, Rangers utilize both their knowledge of the wilds and spellcasting to pursue their prey. Important Ability Scores for Rangers include Dexterity (or Strength ), Constitution , and Wisdom .

- Hit points: At level 1: 10 + Constitution modifier On level up: 6 + Constitution modifier | Key abilities: Dexterity (or Strength) for physical attacks Constitution for Hit Points and Concentration Checks Wisdom for Spells. | Spellcasting ability: Wisdom
- Saving throw proficiencies: Strength, Dexterity
- Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons, Martial weapons Light armour, Medium armour Shields
- Skill proficiencies (Choose 3): Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Investigation, Nature, Perception, Stealth, Survival You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
- Starting Equipment: Shortbow, Leather Armour (green variant), Leather Boots, Arrow of Ice
- Multiclass Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons, Martial weapons Light armour, Medium armour Shields
- Multiclass Skill proficiencies (Choose 1): Same list as Starting Proficiencies

Subclasses:
- **Beast Master** - These Rangers are able to summon an animal companion to aid them in combat and exploration. The companions also gain new features and abilities as the Ranger level increases.
- **Hunter** - These Rangers are more specialized in dealing high damage to enemies, using Spellcasting, and area of effect and utility attacks to hunt down their prey.
- **Gloom Stalker** - Gloom Stalkers specialize in taking out foes swiftly and ruthlessly from the shadows, gaining damage bonuses and increased movement speed.
- **Swarmkeeper** - They command swarms of fey creatures to assist them in battle. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Rogue

Rogues are well versed in the art of stealth and rely on their resourcefulness to be in control of any challenging situation. Rogues use Dexterity as their primary ability score.

- Hit points: At level 1: 8 + Constitution modifier On level up: 5 + Constitution modifier | Key abilities: Dexterity for physical attacks and Armour Class Constitution for Hit Points Intelligence for Spells (Arcane Trickster only) Charisma for "dirty trick" abilities (Swashbuckler only), (exception for sand toss) | Spellcasting ability: Intelligence for the effective use of items, scrolls, and equipment.
- Saving throw proficiencies: Dexterity, Intelligence
- Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons, Hand Crossbows, Longswords, Rapiers, Shortswords Light armour
- Skills with proficiency (Choose 4): Acrobatics, Athletics, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Perception, Performance, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, Stealth You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
- Class Starting Equipment: Shortsword, Leather Armour (purple variant), Leather Boots, Thieves' Tools, Grease Bottle x2
- Multiclass Equipment proficiencies: Light armour
- Multiclass Skills with proficiency (Choose 1): Same list as Starting Proficiencies

Subclasses:
- **Thief** - Most notably, its Fast Hands passive grants a second Bonus Action. Together with the cunning actions the rogue gains at level 2, this subclass allows for exceptional mobility and enhanced versatility.
- **Assassin** - It's focused on dealing higher and more reliable damage to enemies at the start of battle, especially if you can surprise them.
- **Arcane Trickster** - These Rogues are capable of spellcasting (from the Wizard spell list) and use their spells to attack enemies or support allies in combat. Arcane Tricksters use Intelligence as their spellcasting modifier.
- **Swashbuckler** - Unlike other rogues, they focus on fighting enemies head-to-head in single combat. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Sorcerer

Sorcerers use their innate magical powers to fight enemies and aid allies in combat. Sorcerers use Charisma as their primary ability score.

- Hit points: At level 1: 6 + Constitution modifier On level up: 4 + Constitution modifier | Key abilities: Charisma for Spells Constitution for Hit Points and Concentration Checks Dexterity for Armour Class | Spellcasting ability: Charisma
- Saving throw proficiencies: Constitution, Charisma
- Equipment proficiencies: Daggers, Quarterstaves, Light Crossbows
- Skills with proficiency (Choose 2): Arcana, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Persuasion, Religion You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
- Starting Equipment: Quarterstaff, Sorcerer Robe (variant depends on subclass), Simple Boots

Subclasses:
- **Draconic Bloodline** - These Sorcerers are harder to hit than their counterparts. They also have the ability to gain resistance and deal more damage with certain types of damage based on their bloodline.
- **Wild Magic (sorcerer subclass)** - These Sorcerers are capable of using the chaos of their magic to unleash various unexpected effects that can be helpful or chaotic. They also have the ability to gain Advantage on rolls, in exchange for their magic becoming more erratic and surging more often.
- **Storm Sorcery** - They are extremely mobile, being able to fly after each spell they cast, making it safer for them to wade into melee. As they grow in power they can make their spells generate small damaging storms, become resistant to Lightning or Thunder damage and even punish enemies brave enough to attack them by shocking and pushing them far away.
- **Shadow Magic** - They command a shadowy mastiff which can attack and curse enemies. Released as part of Patch 8 in 2025.

## Warlock

Warlocks channel their patron's pact magic to fight enemies and aid allies in combat. Warlocks use Charisma as their primary ability score.

- Hit points: At level 1: 8 + Constitution modifier On level up: 5 + Constitution modifier | Key abilities: Charisma for Spells Constitution for Hit Points and Concentration Dexterity for Armour Class | Spellcasting ability: Charisma
- Saving throw proficiencies: Wisdom, Charisma
- Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons Light armour
- Skills with proficiency (Choose 2): Arcana, Deception, History, Intimidation, Investigation, Nature, Religion You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1, the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
- Starting Equipment: Padded Armour, Dagger, Simple Boots
- Multiclass Equipment proficiencies: Simple weapons Light armour

Subclasses:
- **The Fiend** - These Warlocks are servants of chaotic and destructive beings such as demons or devils and traded their souls to these dark forces in exchange for power. They also have the ability to gain additional Hit Points upon dealing the finishing blow to enemies.
- **The Great Old One** - These Warlocks are acolytes of eldritch entities that are incomprehensible to regular mortals. They are given the ability to channel magic through pacts with these ancient beings in order to control the fabric of reality itself.
- **The Archfey** - Graced by a lady or lord of the fey, these warlocks are imbued with all the sumptuous and fearsome qualities of their patron's extraordinary realm.
- **The Hexblade** - They can curse enemies with their hexed weapons and raise the souls of defeated enemies as spectres. Released as part of Patch 8 on April 15, 2025.

## Wizard

Wizards channel magic through their extensive knowledge of the arcane to fight enemies and aid allies in combat. Wizards use Intelligence as their primary ability score.

- Hit points: At level 1: 6 + Constitution modifier On level up: 4 + Constitution modifier | Spellcasting ability: Intelligence
- Saving throw proficiencies: Intelligence, Wisdom
- Equipment proficiencies: Daggers, Quarterstaves, Light Crossbows
- Skills with proficiency (Choose 2): Arcana, History, Investigation, Insight, Medicine, Religion You receive a bonus to every Skill that you are Proficient in. At character level 1 the Proficiency Bonus is +2.
- Starting Equipment: Quarterstaff, Simple Robe (green variant), Simple Boots

Subclasses:
- **Abjuration School** - These Wizards are specialized in magic that can protect themselves and allies from harm. They also have the ability to reduce the amount of damage dealt to them on subsequent turns whenever they cast an Abjuration spell.
- **Conjuration School** - These Wizards specialize in summoning the elements and moving themselves and others from one place to another.
- **Divination School** - These Wizards specialize in fates and knowledge. They can manipulate random outcomes and see the unseen.
- **Enchantment School** - These Wizards specialize in controlling and befuddling others.
- **Evocation School** - These Wizards are specialized in offensive magic that deal large damage to enemies and control the battlefield. They also have the ability to protect their allies from harmful Evocation spell effects.
- **Illusion School** - These Wizards are specialized in illusory magic that can hide where they are, detect the invisible, or trick enemies into wasting actions.
- **Necromancy School** - These Wizards are specialized in powers of death, allowing them to protect themselves from harm and raise undead minions to serve them.
- **Transmutation School** - These Wizards become accomplished Alchemists, able to pass benefits to companions. At higher levels they can turn into a bird without using a spell slot.
