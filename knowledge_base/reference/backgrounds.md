# Baldur's Gate 3 - Backgrounds

The 12 backgrounds a character can pick at creation (plus the special Haunted One granted to The Dark Urge). Each background grants two skill proficiencies; in BG3 backgrounds do not carry a mechanical feature, only the skills and their inspiration themes. The notes below list who starts with each background by default.

## Acolyte

Acolyte is a character background with skills and inspirations themed around religion. Shadowheart and the hireling Zenith Feur'sel have this background.

- **Insight Proficiency:** Gain proficiency with Wisdom (Insight) ability checks.
- **Religion Proficiency:** Gain proficiency with Intelligence (Religion) ability checks.

## Charlatan

Charlatan is a character background with skills and inspirations themed around trickery and deceit. Astarion and the hireling Kree Derryck have this background.

- **Deception Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Charisma (Deception) Ability Checks.
- **Sleight of Hand Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) Ability Checks.

## Criminal

Criminal is a character background with skills and inspirations themed around law-breaking and the criminal underworld. The hireling Ver'yll Wenkiir has this background.

- **Deception Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Charisma (Deception) Ability Checks.
- **Stealth Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Dexterity (Stealth) Ability Checks.

## Entertainer

Entertainer is a character background with skills and inspirations themed around bardic performances. The hireling Brinna Brightsong has this background.

- **Acrobatics Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Dexterity (Acrobatics) Ability Checks.
- **Performance Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Charisma (Performance) Ability Checks.

## Folk Hero

Folk Hero is a character background with skills and inspirations themed around rescuing others from peril. Wyll, Minsc, and the hireling Danton have this background.

- **Animal Handling Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Wisdom (Animal Handling) Ability Checks.
- **Survival Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Wisdom (Survival) Ability Checks.

## Guild Artisan

Guild Artisan is a character background with skills and inspirations themed around crafting and mercantilism. The hireling Jacelyn has this background.

- **Insight Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Wisdom (Insight) Ability Checks.
- **Persuasion Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Charisma (Persuasion) Ability Checks.

## Haunted One

Haunted One is a character background unique to the Dark Urge with inspirations themed around embracing (or sometimes resisting) The Urge.

- **Medicine Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Wisdom (Medicine) Ability Checks.
- **Intimidation Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Charisma (Intimidation) Ability Checks.

## Noble

Noble is a character background. Minthara and the hireling Kerz have this background.

- **History Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Intelligence (History) Ability Checks.
- **Persuasion Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Charisma (Persuasion) Ability Checks.

## Outlander

Outlander is a character background with skills and inspirations themed around exploration and navigating the wilds. Karlach, Halsin, and the hirelings Eldra Luthrinn and Sina'zith have this background.

- **Athletics Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Strength (Athletics) Ability Checks.
- **Survival Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Wisdom (Survival) Ability Checks.

## Sage

Sage is a character background with skills and inspirations themed around the pursuit of scholarly knowledge. Gale and the hireling Sir Fuzzalump have this background.

- **Arcana Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Intelligence (Arcana) Ability Checks.
- **History Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Intelligence (History) Ability Checks.

## Soldier

Soldier is a character background with skills and inspirations themed around war and masterful combat. Lae'zel, Jaheira, and the hireling Varanna Sunblossom have this background.

- **Athletics Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Strength (Athletics) Ability Checks.
- **Intimidation Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Charisma (Intimidation) Ability Checks.

## Urchin

Urchin is a character background with skills and inspirations themed around street smarts. The hireling Maddala Deadeye has this background.

- **Sleight of Hand Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) Ability Checks.
- **Stealth Proficiency:** Gain Proficiency with Dexterity (Stealth) Ability Checks.
