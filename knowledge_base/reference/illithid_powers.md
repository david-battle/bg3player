# Baldur's Gate 3 - Illithid Powers

The tadpole powers available by consuming Mind Flayer Parasite Specimens, plus elite (Act Three), full-ceremorphosis and story powers. Powers are gained through the power tree at camp; a power's 'Requires' entry lists the prerequisite power (or 'None'). Each tier note below explains when the powers become available.

## Illithid power tree

### Illithid Persuasion

- Type: Passive Feature
- Requires: Unlocked automatically
- You can use your connection to the parasite in your brain to force cultists of the Absolute to obey you. Recharge: Long rest

### Psionic Overload

- Type: Class Action
- Your attacks deal an additional 1d4 Psychic damage, but you take 1d4 Psychic damage every turn. Recharge: Short rest

### Favourable Beginnings

- Type: Passive Feature
- The first Attack Roll or Ability Check you make against any target gains a bonus equal to your Proficiency Bonus.

### Force Tunnel

- Type: Class Action
- Charge forward, pushing all objects and creatures in your path 4 m / 13 ft away from you. Recharge: Short rest

### Concentrated Blast

- Type: Class Action
- Deal 3d6 Psychic damage. You must be Concentrating on another spell to cast this. If the target was concentrating, you heal as much as the damage that was dealt to it. The spell you were concentrating on will end. Recharge: Per turn

### Transfuse Health

- Type: Class Action
- Sacrifice half your remaining hit points to heal a target for the same amount. Recharge: Per turn

### Stage Fright

- Type: Class Action
- Requires: Psionic Overload
- Your targets have Disadvantage on Attack Rolls and take 2d6 Psychic damage each time they miss. Targets overcome their Stage Fright early when they succeed on an Attack Roll. Recharge: Short rest

### Ability Drain

- Type: Passive Feature
- Requires: Psionic Overload
- Once per turn, when you make an Attack Roll, the attack reduces that target's corresponding Ability by 1. The Ability that is reduced is the same as the one used to make the Attack Roll. Recharge: Per turn

### Luck of the Far Realms

- Type: Reaction
- Requires: Favourable Beginnings
- When you make a successful Attack Roll against a foe, you can change that attack into a Critical Hit. Recharge: Long rest

### Charm

- Type: Reaction
- Requires: Favourable Beginnings
- Channel the dark allure of the tadpole to Charm an enemy that attacks you, preventing them from attacking you until their next turn. Note: Enemies have Advantage on the Saving Throw against being charmed.

### Displace

- Type: Passive Feature
- Requires: Force Tunnel
- Creatures suffering Falling damage because of your actions take an additional 1d8 Psychic damage.

### Repulsor

- Type: Class Action
- Requires: Force Tunnel
- Push anything and anyone back 6 m / 20 ft. Deals 2d6 Force damage, targets take half damage if successful on save. Recharge: Short rest

### Psionic Backlash

- Type: Reaction
- Requires: Concentrated Blast
- When an enemy within 9 m / 30 ft casts a spell, you can use your Reaction to inflict 1d4 Psychic damage to the caster per the spell‘s level.

### Cull the Weak

- Type: Toggleable Passive Feature
- Requires: Concentrated Blast
- When you bring a creature down to fewer hit points than your number of evolved illithid powers, it dies and all nearby creatures take 1~4 Psychic damage.

### Shield of Thralls

- Type: Class Action
- Requires: Transfuse Health
- Conjure a volatile shield around yourself or an ally, granting the target 10 temporary hit points. Recharge: Short rest

### Perilous Stakes

- Type: Class Action
- Requires: Transfuse Health
- Invest a creature with power that heals it when it attacks, but also makes it vulnerable to all damage. Recharge: Long rest

## Elite powers

Upon first gaining access to illithid powers, the outer ring of abilities are locked. At the very end of Act Two , on the road to Baldur's Gate , the party is given the opportunity to unlock these powers by agreeing to absorb an Astral-Touched Tadpole and become a partial-illithid . Doing this automatically unlocks Fly (Illithid Power) and the 5 powers of the innermost ring: Concentrated Blast Favourable Beginnings Force Tunnel Psionic Overload , and Transfuse Health . Any regular Tadpoles the character might have already spent to unlock these abilities will be refunded. Absorbing the Astral-Touched Tadpole causes the character to undergo Partial Ceremorphosis and irrevocably changes their appearance: their eyes darken and their entire body covers with dark veins. ### Fracture Psyche

- Type: Class Action
- Requires: Unlock one of: Ability Drain Illithid Expertise
- Invade a target's mind and disrupt its defences. The target's Armour Class is reduced by 1. If the target dies while its psyche is fractured, you can cast Shatter Psyche on another target. Recharge: Short rest

### Illithid Expertise

- Type: Passive Feature
- Requires: Unlock one of: Luck of the Far Realms Fracture Psyche
- You have deepened your sense of self, gaining Expertise in Persuasion, Deception and Intimidation checks. Also grants Proficiency in those skills if not already possessed.

### Psionic Dominance

- Type: Reaction
- Requires: Unlock one of: Charm Black Hole
- Use your Reaction to cancel a spell targeted at you if its spell level is less than or equal to your proficiency bonus. Recharge: Long rest

### Black Hole

- Type: Class Action
- Requires: Unlock one of: Displace Psionic Dominance
- Summon a black hole that pulls in nearby enemies and potentially Slows them. Up to five more black holes can be summoned after first casting. Recharge: Short rest

### Fly

- Type: Class Action
- Requires: Unlocked automatically
- Fly to a target position. Consumes Movement Speed speed but not an Action or Bonus Action.

### Mind Blast

- Type: Class Action
- Spew forth a conical wave of psychic energy and possibly Stun targets within. Deals 4d8+Spellcasting Ability Modifier Psychic damage. Recharge: Long rest

### Mind Sanctuary

- Type: Class Action
- Requires: Unlock one of: Psionic Backlash Freecast
- Sculpt a magical nexus that allows those within to take Actions and Bonus Actions interchangeably. Recharge: Long rest

### Freecast

- Type: Toggleable Passive Feature
- Requires: Unlock one of: Shield of Thralls Mind Sanctuary
- You have discovered a marvellous adaptability within yourself. Spell slots, charges, and similar resource costs for your next action or spell are removed. Recharge: Long rest

### Absorb Intellect

- Type: Class Action
- Requires: Unlock one of: Perilous Stakes Displacer Beast Shape
- Gobble up a foe's intellect, lowering their Intelligence by 1 per turn and healing your wounds for 5 turns (1d8 Hit Points each turn). Recharge: Short rest

### Displacer Beast Shape

- Type: Class Action
- Requires: Unlock one of: Stage Fright Absorb Intellect
- Transform into a displacer beast that can Displace itself and enemies, and has 85 hit points. You take on the attributes of a displacer beast, but maintain your Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores. When your displacer beast form drops to 0 hit points, you revert to your original form. Recharge: Long rest

## Full ceremorphosis

During the final quest, Confront the Elder Brain , the player character will have the option to undergo full ceremorphosis and become a full-illithid . At this point, they will automatically unlock most illithid powers and gain access to more powerful versions of a select few. These abilities are not available through the "Illithid Power" screen, but are instead granted automatically after transformation.

### Strengthened Force Tunnel

- Type: Class Action
- Charge forward, pushing all objects and creatures in your path 12 m / 40 ft away from you. Deals 3d10+4 Force and may cause Prone

### Fierce Perilous Stakes

- Type: Class Action
- Grant an ally an additional 15 Psychic damage, automatic Critical Hit when they roll a 15 or higher, and healing on hit. However, they will also be vulnerable to all damage.

### Nebulous Black Hole

- Type: Class Action
- Create a point of intense gravity dealing 3d10+4 Force damage and possibly slowing enemies.

### Synaptic Consumption

- Type: Class Action
- Lower all of an enemy's ability scores by 5, then 1 per turn, and regain 2~20 Hit Points per turn for five turns. The foe is left Prone.

### Augmented Shield of Thralls

- Type: Class Action
- Target two creatures, granting them 25 temporary hit points. Once these hit points are diminished, the shield bursts and leaves nearby enemies Stunned.

### Potent Concentrated Blast

- Type: Class Action
- Deals 9d6 Psychic damage to an enemy. If they were Concentrating on a spell, heal an equal amount of hit points.

### Extract Brain

- Type: Class Action
- Lobotomise a nearby creature that is Stunned, Prone, Sleeping, or Unconscious, dealing 5d10+50 Piercing damage and regaining 6d6 Hit Points.

### Tentacle Whip

- Type: Class Action
- Deal 3d10+5 Psychic melee damage, and possibly Stun the target.

### Levitate

- Type: Class Action
- Float to a destination using Movement Speed speed.

### Fracture Psyche

- Type: Class Action
- Invade a target's mind and disrupt its defences. The target's Armour Class and Saving throws are reduced by 5. If the target dies while its psyche is fractured, you can cast Shatter Psyche on another target. Recharge: Short rest

### Permanent Mind Sanctuary

- Type: Passive Feature
- Allows creature to take Actions and Bonus Actions interchangeably.

## Other powers

These illithid powers are not part of the illithid power tree and are instead unlocked through story events.

### Survival Instinct

- Type: Class Action
- Requires: Reward from: Help Omeluum Investigate the Parasite
- Infuse a creature with psionic force. If it reaches 0 hit points, it regains 3d4 Hit Points instead of falling Unconscious. Recharge: Short rest

### Awakened

- Type: Passive Feature
- Requires: Succeed on all three saving throws when interacting with the Zaith'isk in Crèche Y'llek. Or, if Lae'zel is in the Zaith'isk, succeed on the final ability check.
- You can use all of your Illithid Powers as a Bonus Action. Your resistance to the zaith'isk in the githyanki infirmary awakened this power.

## Spellcasting ability

Main article: Spellcasting ability Some illithid powers involve saving throws . For these powers, the difficulty class is determined by your spellcasting ability modifier , even for classes that do not typically have spells. All bonuses to spell save DC such as Arcane Acuity or Arcane Enchantment will also apply to illithid powers. If multiclassing, the ability score used by this modifier is determined by the latest class added. In other words, taking level 1 in a new class will switch the spellcasting ability modifier used by illthid powers (and items or scrolls) to the modifier of the new class.
