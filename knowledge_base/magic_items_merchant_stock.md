### Dagger +1
- Rarity: Uncommon | Slot: Weapon (Daggers) | Act: Act One, Act Two, Act Three
- Description: Dagger +1 is an uncommon, lightly enchanted (+1) variant of the Daggers family of weapons. It is a simple melee weapon wielded in one hand. Its design lends itself well to be thrown at enemies as a projectile.
- Effect: Damage: 1d4 + 1 (2~5) + Strength or Dexterity modifier Piercing | Piercing Strike: Stab an enemy and possibly inflict Gaping Wounds. (Recharge: Short rest.)
- How to get:
  - Sold by any trader using the magic melee table
  - On a small hill overlooking the barn at the Blighted Village, stuck into a piece of meat roasting over a campfire
- Notes:
  - The dagger can be removed from the piece of meat by passing one of the following checks: [SLEIGHT OF HAND] Carefully wiggle the dagger until it slides free. (DC 15)
  - [STRENGTH] Grasp the handle and pull the dagger with all your might. (DC 15)
  - [INVESTIGATION] Examine the meat. Perhaps there is some trick to removing the dagger. (DC 15) Align the dagger with the meat's grain and pull.

### Flail +1
- Rarity: Uncommon | Slot: Weapon (Flails) | Act: Act One, Act Two, Act Three
- Description: Flail +1 is an uncommon, lightly enchanted (+1) variant of the Flails family of weapons. It is a martial melee weapon wielded in one hand.
- Effect: Damage: 1d8 + 1 (2~9) + Strength modifier Bludgeoning | Tenacity: When you miss a melee attack, you deal Strength Modifier Bludgeoning damage (minimum of 1) anyway. | Concussive Smash: Hit an enemy with all your might to deal damage and possibly Daze them. (Recharge: Short rest.) | Weakening Strike: Target an enemy's hands with a non-lethal attack and possibly inflict Weak Grip. (Recharge: Short rest.)
- How to get:
  - Sold by any trader using the magic melee table
- Notes:
  - If a weapon action misses, using Tenacity does not trigger its effects.
  - As Tenacity deals weapon damage even if an attack misses, it can trigger many on-hit effects from equipment. For example, from the Gloves of Power, the Callous Glow Ring, weapon coatings, or any infusion from the Ring of Elemental Infusion.
- Bugs:
  - When dual-wielding a flail or morningstar with a non-Tenacity weapon using the Dual Wielder feat, Tenacity can be triggered by missing an attack with the non-Tenacity weapon, dealing Bludgeoning damage regardless of the weapon used. Some non-Tenacity weapons can trigger their on-hit effects via Tenacity as well, such as the Ritual Dagger and Loviatar's Scourge.
  - As Horde Breaker (Melee) rolls invisible, zero-damage weapon attacks against all enemies in the Horde Breaker area of effect, Tenacity can be prompted to trigger against them even if the first attack hits. It is therefore possible to score 2 hits in one attack: the full attack against the original Horde Breaker target and a Tenacity hit against another target.
