### Wavemother's Boots
- Rarity: Rare | Slot: Boots | Act: Act Three
- Description: Wavemother's Boots is a rare pair of Boots that allow the wearer to heal while standing in water.
- Effect: Watery Rejuvenation: If the wearer is standing in a Water surface at the start of their turn, they heal 1d4 Hit Points.
- Notes:
  - Watery Rejuvenation only triggers if the wearer is in combat and standing on an actual Water surface. It does not trigger when standing in a permanent body of water, such as can be found in the Lower City Sewers, nor does it trigger on standing in other types of liquid surfaces such as Blood or Alcohol.
  - Hit points can be repeatedly regained outside of combat by entering turn-based mode while standing in a water surface and repeatedly ending the turn until it is the wearer's next turn.

### Amulet of Elemental Torment
- Rarity: Uncommon | Slot: Amulet | Act: Act Three
- Description: The Amulet of Elemental Torment is an uncommon Amulet that channels the effects of certain Surfaces into the wearer's spells.
- Effect: Nature's Vengeance: When the wearer stands on certain surfaces and casts a spell that deals damage, the target also suffers the surface's effects.
- How to get:
  - House of Hope X: -6477 Y: 2884: Carried by Hope while fighting Raphael in the Foyer
- Notes:
  - Previously was sold by Grat the Trader in the Goblin Camp, Brem in the Zhentarim Basement, or Roah Moonglow in Shattered Sanctum. As of Patch 3, this was moved to Hope in the House of Hope.
  - The surface effects applied by Nature's Vengeance are: Fire: Apply Burning for 2 turns. Deals the first tick of 1d4 Fire damage immediately.
  - Caustic Brine: Apply Caustic Brine for 2 turns. Deals the first tick of 1d4 Acid damage immediately.
  - Simple Toxin: Apply Poisoned for 2 turns.
  - Acid: Apply Acid for 2 turns.
  - Nature's Vengeance does not work with any other surfaces. This includes the other basic elemental surfaces like Ice surfaces or Electrified Water surfaces.
  - Valid poison surfaces can be created by breaking Simple Toxin vials or with Chromatic Orb. Surfaces created by breaking stronger poisons like Serpent Fang Toxin are different and do not work.

### Cloth Armour
- Rarity: Uncommon | Slot: Clothing | Act: Act Three
- Description: Cloth Armour is an uncommon piece of Clothing that grants Wrath when an enemy damages the wearer.
- Effect: AC 10 | Grievous Retribution: When an enemy damages the wearer, the wearer gains Wrath for 2 turns.
- How to get:
  - Heapside Prison X: -1266 Y: 744: On a table in the south-eastern room
- Notes:
  - Despite the inherent contradiction in its name, this item is treated as clothing by the game engine, and does not count as armour for the purposes of effects that trigger when wearing (or not wearing) armour.
  - Grievous Retribution can be activated only once per turn.
- Bugs:
  - The in game tooltip incorrectly states Grievous Retribution grants 1 turn of Wrath; it actually grants 2 turns of the condition.

### Kill Collar
- Rarity: Uncommon | Slot: Amulet | Act: Act Three
- Description: Kill Collar is an uncommon Amulet that represents the collars used by Gortash to keep the Gondians within the Iron Foundry in check.
- How to get:
  - Carried by Zanner Toobin in the Steel Watch Foundry, Lower City
- Notes:
  - Despite wearing the collars, no other Gondian will drop one.
  - If a character has this collar equipped, is within the Lower Foundry area of the Steel Watch Foundry, and a Black Fist's Motivator is allowed to activate then the character wearing the collar will explode and be instantly killed. If a Motivator is allowed to activate under any other circumstances then the collar will instead be replaced with an unequippable Destroyed Kill Collar.
  - Despite the Gondians having a visible model of a collar this item has no model for the player.
