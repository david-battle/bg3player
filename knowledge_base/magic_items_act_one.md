Full reference for every magic equipment item obtainable in this act, sorted by in-game location. Coordinates (X, Y) are in-game map coordinates.

### Sentient Amulet (Rare)
- Rarity: Rare | Slot: Amulet | Act: Act One
- Description: Sentient Amulet is a rare Amulet that allows the wearer to cast Shatter once per Short Rest (with a drawback) and Ki Restoration (Lesser) once per Long Rest.
- Effect: Shatter: Cast as a level 2 spell (Recharge: Short rest.) | Talk to the Sentient Amulet: Speak with the consciousness lingering inside the Sentient Amulet. | Ki Restoration: Using the spiritual energy in the amulet, you restore 2 Ki Points. (Recharge: Long rest.) | High Spirits: The amulet's spirit lingers, making everything seem a little more vibrant. Must succeed a Wisdom Saving Throw (DC 10/15/20, see notes) when using the amulet's power, or become Hysterical (equivalent in effect to Tasha's Hideous Laughter with a duration of 1 turn).
- How to get:
  - Abandoned Refuge X: -624 Y: 224: In a locked Adamantine Chest on the island with the broken structure near the Lava Elemental in Grymforge
  - In a locked Adamantine Chest on the island with the broken structure near the Lava Elemental in Grymforge
- Notes:
  - Picking up the amulet triggers a conversation in which the amulet tries to get party member to surrender to a fit of laughter. This is a hint at the curse the amulet bears. Once the conversation is over, the quest Help the Cursed Monk is received.
  - The amulet is voiced by John O'Mahony.
  - Wisdom Saving Throws are only required when using this amulet to cast Shatter. Using the Ki Restoration (Lesser) ability does not prompt a Wisdom save.
  - The default Wisdom Saving Throw for Hysterical is a DC 10. However, after succeeding the Saving Throw at least three times and upon either equipping the amulet or leaving camp (whichever happens first), a special cutscene plays where passing a DC 10 Wisdom Saving Throw to resist the curse is necessary. If failed, the Hysterical DC is increased to 15. If the Wisdom Saving Throw for Hysterical is succeeded a further three times (and then equipping the amulet or leaving camp again), a similar cutscene plays whereby failing a DC 15 Wisdom Saving Throw increases the Hysterical DC to 20. After this, no additional cutscenes will occur.
  - Depending on the outcome of the Help the Spirit of the Amulet quest in Act Three, the party may receive a version of this amulet that is visually identical but missing all of its special properties (UID: UNI_WYR_Monk Amulet_No Ghost / UUID: 14a6da9a-6689-4c70-96a1-d03319ba3278). Alternatively, the player may receive the Sentient Amulet (Very Rare).
  - While the player character carries the Sentient Amulet, the Cursed Monk occasionally makes comments at various locations and after specific interactions.

### Club of Hill Giant Strength
- Rarity: Uncommon | Slot: Weapon (Clubs) | Act: Act One
- Description: Club of Hill Giant Strength is an uncommon club that that sets the wearer's Strength to 19.
- Effect: Damage: 1d4 (1~4) + Strength modifier Bludgeoning | Splinters of a Giant's Might: Increases Strength to 19. | Concussive Smash: Hit an enemy with all your might to deal damage and possibly Daze them. (Recharge: Short rest.)
- How to get:
  - Arcane Tower X: -26 Y: -272: Obtained by breaking the Stool of Hill Giant Strength
  - Obtained by breaking the Stool of Hill Giant Strength
- Notes:
  - It is possible to equip this Club in a character's offhand and keep their main hand slot free (e.g. for brawler monk):
  - Equip a dagger in the character's main hand and this club in their offhand.
  - Switch to another companion with no weapons equipped.
  - Click on their weapon slot and choose to equip the mainhand weapon currently in use by the first character. They will swap the weapon in the first character's primary weapon slot to their empty weapon slot, leaving the first character with the club in their offhand, yet with nothing in their main hand.
  - According to D&D rules, this item should be named Club of Ogre Strength, because Ogres have STR = 19 while Hill Giants have STR = 21.
  - The in-game model has the word "STRENGTH" engraved on it in the Dethek runic script.
  - Patch 1 changed the Strength increase from 15 to 19.

### Staff of Arcane Blessing
- Rarity: Uncommon | Slot: Weapon (Quarterstaves) | Act: Act One
- Description: Staff of Arcane Blessing is an uncommon Quarterstaff which grants the wielder the ability to cast Bless. In addition, all Bless spells cast by the wielder grant an additional +1d4 to spell attack rolls.
- Effect: One-handed damage: 1d6 (1~6) + Strength modifier Bludgeoning | Two-handed damage: 1d8 (1~8) + Strength modifier Bludgeoning | Bless: Cast as a level 1 spell (Recharge: Long rest.) | Mystra's Blessing: Creatures you Bless also gain Mystra's Blessing for an additional 1d4 bonus to spell attack rolls. | Topple: Swipe at a creature to knock it Prone. (Recharge: Short rest.)
- How to get:
  - Arcane Tower X: 164 Y: 432: Leaning against a table in the basement
  - Leaning against a table in the basement
- Notes:
  - To reach the basement, the player must either convince Bernard on the top floor to leave Guiding Light on a table, or defeat Bernard and take it. The ring can be used to activate a secret button on the tower's teleporter pad to reach the basement.
  - Another way to reach the basement of the Arcane Tower is to go up the stairs behind the generator, use Feather Fall, and jump down to the gap between the wall and the wooden staircase.

  - The in-game description of this passive feature is not entirely correct, and reads as follows:
  - Instead, creatures Blessed with the Staff of Arcane Blessing gain two separate conditions: The regular Bless condition, which grants a 1d4 bonus to all saving throws and attack rolls.

In this case, the creature is left with a 1d4 bonus to spell attack rolls, but loses the other 1d4 bonuses, despite the tooltip of the condition claiming otherwise.
  - Mystra's Blessing is only applied when Bless is cast using a level 1 or level 2 spell slot, in addition to the once-per-day version granted by the staff itself.
