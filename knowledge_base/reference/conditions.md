# Baldur's Gate 3 - Conditions Glossary

Core gameplay conditions that come up when evaluating gear, spells and consumables. Effects and sources are taken from each condition's bg3.wiki page. This is a curated core list, not the full 1,500+ condition pages on the wiki. Some common conditions (e.g. Blessed, Weakened, Deafened, Exhaustion) have no dedicated wiki page and are omitted rather than guessed.

## Conditions (135)

### Acid Resistance (Condition)

- **Effect:** Resistant to Acid damage.
- Sources: Elixir of Acid Resistance

### Aid (Condition)

- **Effect:** Hit point maximum increased by 5 Hit Points.
- **Effect:** Hit point maximum increases by a further 5 Hit Points for every Spell Slot Level above 2 used.
- Sources: Scroll of Aid, Absolute's Talisman: Aid, Aid, Khalid's Gift: Aid, Shield of Devotion: Aid
- **Note:** The Aid condition remains even if the caster is removed from the party.

### Arcane Acuity (Condition)

- **Effect:** Affected entity has a +1 bonus to its spell Attack Rolls and spell save DC for each remaining turn.
- **Effect:** Reduce the duration by 2 each time the entity takes damage.
- **Effect:** Arcane Acuity has a maximum Duration: 10 turns.
- Sources: Elixir of Battlemage's Power, Gloves of Battlemage's Power, Hat of Fire Acuity, Hat of Storm Scion's Power, Helmet of Arcane Acuity, Battle Acuity, Battlemage's Power, Fire Acuity, Thunderous Acuity
- **Note:** The duration is reduced by 1 at the beginning of each turn.

### Arcane Charge (Condition)

- **Effect:** While damaging Threatened entities, deal +2 bonus damage with spells.[See: Bugs]
- Sources: Bided Time, Boots of Arcane Bolstering, Accretion, Rapid Caster, Time Thoroughly Bided
- **Note:** If a single attack does multiple independent instances of damage, Arcane Charge boosts each one, so spells like Magic Missile benefit the most.
- **Note:** Some damage riders behave as independent sources of damage and get boosted by Arcane Charge. See DRS effects for a comprehensive list of damage riders that work like this.

### Arcane Synergy (Condition)

- **Effect:** Weapon attacks deal additional damage equal to affected entity's Spell Casting Ability Modifier.
- Sources: Synergetic Cantrips, Synergetic Moments
- **Note:** Only applies on weapon attack rolls – e.g., Flame Blade is treated as a spell attack roll, and so does not apply.
- **Note:** This applies in addition to the standard damage modifier, so the spellcasting ability can increase damage multiple times if Infernal Rapier, Sylvan Scimitar, Shillelagh, Pact of the Blade, or similar effects are in use.

### Armour of Agathys (Condition)

- **Effect:** Gained 5 × spell level temporary hit points. If hit with a melee attack, the attacker takes 5 × spell level Cold damage. This effect ends when the temporary hit points are lost.
- Sources: Armour of Agathys, Magic Allergy: Ice Bloom

### Arms of Hadar (Condition)

- **Effect:** Can't take Reactions.
- Sources: Arms of Hadar

### Aura of Conquest (Condition)

- **Effect:** Frightened enemies within 3 m (10 ft) take 5 Psychic damage per turn.
- Sources: Har'rak

### Aura of Courage (Condition)

- **Effect:** The paladin and any nearby allies can't be Frightened.
- **Effect:** Can't be Frightened within 3 m (10 ft) of the spellcaster.
- Sources: Aura of Courage (Aura) (Condition)

### Aura of Devotion (Condition)

- **Effect:** The paladin and any nearby allies can't be Charmed.
- **Effect:** Can't be Charmed within 3 m (10 ft) of the spellcaster.
- Sources: Aura of Devotion (Aura) (Condition)

### Aura of Hate (Condition)

- **Effect:** This creature and all fiends and undead within 3 m (10 ft) gain this creature's charisma modifier as a bonus to their melee weapon damage.
- **Effect:** Gains a bonus to melee weapon damage equal to the charisma modifier of the creature emanating the aura.
- Sources: Aura of Hate (Aura) (Condition)
- **Note:** The effect does not apply to unarmed attacks such as with claws.
- **Note:** The effect really applies to all nearby fiends and undead, including enemies.

### Aura of Protection (Condition)

- **Effect:** The creature and its allies within 3 m (10 ft) gain the creature's charisma modifier as a bonus to their saving throws.
- **Effect:** Gains a bonus to saving throws equal to the charisma modifier of the creature emanating the aura.
- Sources: Aura of Protection (Aura) (Condition)

### Aura of Terror (Condition)

- **Effect:** Nearby enemies have Disadvantage on Attack Rolls and Saving Throws. Creatures that can't be Frightened are immune to this aura.
- **Effect:** Bane's oppressive aura gives the affected entity Disadvantage on attack rolls and Saving Throws while within 3 m (10 ft) of the spellcaster.
- Sources: Aura of Terror (Aura) (Condition)

### Bane (Condition)

- **Effect:** Has a -1d4 penalty to Attack Rolls and Saving Throws.
- Sources: Coated in Oil of Bane (Condition), Gloves of Power, Oil of Bane, Absolute's Bane, Baneful Bond, Haroldish Doom, The Scourge Mistress' Bane, Bane (spell), Hordestrike, Profane Scourge

- **Effect:** Banished from this plane of existence.

### Barkskin (Condition)

- **Effect:** Armour Class increased to 16.
- Sources: Barkskin
- **Note:** If your Armour Class is lower than 16, Barkskin changes your base Armour Class to 16 with no other bonuses allowed from any source.
- **Note:** Mage Armour, Draconic Resilience, Unarmoured Defence (Barbarian), Unarmoured Defence (Monk) and Barkskin all change your base armour class and therefore do not stack. If you have multiple of these you will only get the benefit of the one that gives the most Armour Class.
- **Note:** Barkskin can work while in Wild Shape. This effect only works on forms with an Armour Class of less than 16.

- **Effect:** Has Disadvantage on Attack Rolls. Range of attacks and spells reduced to 3 m (10 ft). Attack Rolls against Blinded creatures have Advantage.
- **Effect:** The affected creature has Disadvantage on Attack Rolls. Ranged attacks and spells of the affected creature have a range of 3 m (10 ft). Attack Rolls against affected creatures have Advantage. At the end of each turn, the affected creature makes a Constitution Saving Throw. If successful, the condition is removed.

- **Note:** Being Blinded has no effect on the range of Throw or any other action with the IgnoreVisionBlock flag.

### Blindness (Condition)

- **Effect:** Has Disadvantage on Attack Rolls. Range of attacks and spells reduced to 3 m (10 ft).
- **Effect:** Attack Rolls against Blinded creatures have Advantage.
- **Effect:** At the end of each turn, the affected creature rolls a Constitution Saving Throw against the Spell Save DC of the caster. If successful, the condition is removed.

- **Effect:** Has Advantage on Strength Checks. Weight Limit doubled.

### Burning (Condition)

- **Effect:** Takes 1d4 Fire damage per turn. Can be removed with the Help action, using a Healing Potion or gaining Wet. Immune from if Wet. Dip action can be used on Burning characters or objects.
- Sources: Fire (surface), Wall of Fire (area), Alchemist's Fire, Arrow of Fire, Cindermoth Cloak, Flammable Slime Bomb, Ochre Jelly Slime, Blazing Hide, Cauteriser, Elements of an Epoch, Fiery Return, Flaming Shroud, Hellstalker, Hot Little Hands, Legendary Action: Draconic Fury, Nature's Vengeance, Unfettered Fire, Alchemist's Fire (action), Burning Overflow, Fire Breath (Dragons), Igniting Spark, Multiattack (Red Dragon), Smouldering Touch, Recast Dazzling Ray, Scorching Strike, Searing Blood
- **Note:** Conditions that share the same StackID prevent the additional application of these conditions until the original condition has timed out. As a result, a character that is affected by Burning will not also be affected by Melting, meaning this character may walk around freely in lava while only receiving 1d4 Fire damage instead of 10d6 Fire damage.
- **Note:** Burning creatures take double damage from the Fire Elemental's Multiattack, and are made Brittle by the Water Elemental's Winter's Breath.
- **Note:** Burning creatures take an additional 1d4 Weapon damage when attacked by Dragon's Grasp or Firestoker.

- **Effect:** Has Advantage on Dexterity Checks. Only takes half damage from falling.

### Charmed (Condition)

- **Effect:** Cannot attack the spellcaster. The spellcaster has Advantage on Charisma Checks in dialogue.

### Chilled (Condition)

- **Effect:** Affected entity is vulnerable to Cold damage and resistant to Fire damage.
- **Effect:** Applying Wet to a chilled entity will make it Frozen for Duration: 1 turn.
- Sources: Chilling Counter, Elements of an Epoch, Insidious Cold, Cold Embrace, Icy Regurgitation, Slam (Water Elemental), Hiemal Strike
- **Note:** The order of application of conditions matters. Chilling an already wet target will not freeze it. It must be chilled and then wet. However, applying wet to an already wet and chilled target will freeze it.
- **Note:** The duration of the Frozen condition ticks down at the start of the affected creature's turn. With a duration of just 1 turn, this means the effect will immediately end at the start of the frozen creature's turn without disabling it for even a single turn. When applied with Encrusted with Frost, Frozen will instead last 2 turns (so effectively 1 full round), so it does not have this issue.

### Dominated (Condition)

- **Effect:** Affected entity will follow and fight alongside the caster of this spell. Every time it takes damage, it may break the caster's control over it with a successful Wisdom Saving Throw.
- **Effect:** Affected entity will follow and fight alongside the caster of this spell. Every time it takes damage, it may break the caster's control over it with a successful Wisdom Saving Throw.

### Downed (Condition)

- **Effect:** If you reach 0 hit points, you fall Unconscious and must make Death Saving Throws. On 3 successes, you stop bleeding out and become Stable. On 3 failures, you die. Any damage you receive while unconscious counts as one failure. If you regain any Hit Points, the condition is removed. If an ally helps you, you regain 1 hit point.

### Drunk (Condition)

- **Effect:** Has Disadvantage on Dexterity and Charisma Checks.
- Sources: Drunken Inhale, Intoxicating Strike

### Dying (Condition)

- **Effect:** This creature is dying. It will not move, take actions, or respond to damage in any way.
- **Effect:** Attacks at melee range are guaranteed to hit and be critical hits. Ranged attacks have Advantage.
- Sources: Mind flayer (nautiloid wreck)

### Encrusted with Frost (Condition)

- **Effect:** Affected entity has Disadvantage on Dexterity Saving Throws.
- **Effect:** When there are 7 or more turns remaining, the entity must succeed a DC 12 Constitution Saving Throw or take 1d4 ColdDRS and become Frozen. On a successful save, it only takes half damage. Afterward, the frost sloughs away.
- **Effect:** Removed by Burning.
- Sources: Coated in Oil of Freezing (Condition), Conduit of Kereska's Ice (Condition), Oil of Freezing, Coldbrim Chill, Winter's Clutches (passive feature)
- **Note:** Markoheshkir's Encrusted with Frost condition comes from the Frost of Dark Winter variant of Kereska's Favour.
- **Note:** Testing as of Patch 7 confirms that the save is fixed DC 12, despite the combat log showing a variable DC calculation involving Proficiency and Spellcasting Ability Modifier bonuses.

### Hastened (Condition)

- **Effect:** Creature has a +2 bonus to Armour Class, Advantage on Dexterity Saving Throws, its Movement Speed increases by 9 m (30 ft),[See: Bugs] and it can take one additional Action per turn. When the condition ends, the creature becomes Lethargic.

This is because for honour mode specifically, these conditions are assigned the HASTE stack ID and a higher stack priority than Hastened. Mind Sanctuary: Haste also ends Hastened in honour mode, potentially causing creatures to accidentally become Lethargic.

### Threatened (Condition)

- **Effect:** An enemy is close. Has Disadvantage on ranged Attack Rolls.
- **Note:** A creature's melee range can be affected by the reach of their melee weapon.
- **Note:** The condition itself has no effect on attacks against the threatening enemy; the disadvantage only applies to ranged attacks against other creatures. If multiple enemies are in threatening range, then the Threatened creature will have disadvantage against all of them.
- **Note:** However, there is also a separate source of disadvantage ("Target is too close") when making a ranged attack from within 3 m (10 ft) of a target, meaning most creatures will have disadvantage on ranged attacks against a threatening creature. This can be removed by Crossbow Expert: Point-Blank, making it possible to use crossbow attacks without disadvantage against an enemy at close range. This does not work, however, if one is Threatened by multiple creatures.
- **Note:** Because of how the "Target is too close" effect is implemented, it is possible for a target to be within an aura with a range of 3 m (10 ft) (such as Aura of Murder) while not being close enough to grant disadvantage on ranged attacks. This is because the effect measures the distance from the centre of the attacker to the target, while an aura accounts for the size of a creature's hitbox.
