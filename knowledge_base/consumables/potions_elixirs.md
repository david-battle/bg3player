# Baldur's Gate 3 - Potions and Elixirs

All potions and elixirs (including mundane ones), with effects, duration info, and where to get them. Effects last until a long rest unless stated otherwise; elixirs replace each other's effects when drunk.

Acts: Act One / Act Two / Act Three. 'Act: unknown' = no specific vendor/location documented on bg3.wiki (often sold throughout the game or found as random loot).
