# Baldur's Gate 3 - Alchemy

Crafting reference: combine **3 ingredients** to make an **extract**, then combine one **specific extract** with any extract of a second **generic family** to craft a potion, elixir, grenade or coating. Craft/Trade Levels gate which characters and traders can craft/sell the result.

Rarity: Common for ingredients/extracts; consumable results are mostly Common/Uncommon.


## Ingredients and where to get them

First vendor = earliest-act trader that sells the ingredient, so the AI can point a player at the first place to stock up. Most ingredients are also random world loot; world locations are not tracked here.
