# Baldur's Gate 3 - Scrolls

All 137 spell scrolls, with the spell effect and where to get them. Scrolls can be used by any class; Wizards can learn the spell from a scroll for a gold cost.


## Act One

### Scroll of Melf's Acid Arrow
- Type: Scroll | Rarity: Uncommon | Act: Act One
- Effect: Casts Melf's Acid Arrow
- How to get:
  - Sold by traders, found in containers, and carried by enemies
  - Inside Gut's chambers on the corridor floor in the Shattered Sanctum

### Scroll of Misty Step
- Type: Scroll | Rarity: Uncommon | Act: Act One
- Effect: Move to a visible unoccupied space without triggering reactions Range: 18 m (60 ft)
- How to get:
  - Sold by traders, found in containers, and carried by enemies
  - In Baelen's Pack in the Underdark

### Scroll of Speak with Dead
- Type: Scroll | Rarity: Uncommon | Act: Act One
- Effect: Casts Speak with Dead. Range: 18 m (60 ft) | Grants the ability to recast Speak with Dead. Range: 18 m (60 ft) | Duration: Until Long rest
- How to get:
  - Sold by traders, found in containers, and carried by enemies
  - Inside the Goblin Camp, next to Dror Ragzlin and the dead Mind Flayer

### Scroll of Aid
- Type: Scroll | Rarity: Common | Act: Act One, Act Three
- Effect: Restores 5 Hit Points and grants Aid Range: Self | AoE: 9 m (30 ft) Radius
- How to get:
  - On a skeleton at X: 407 Y: 22, down the stairs from Gut's bedchamber in the Defiled Temple
  - Carried by Lurgan in the Decrepit Village
  - In a stack of scrolls in The Counting House

### Scroll of Chromatic Orb
- Type: Scroll | Rarity: Common | Act: Act One
- Effect: Deal 3d8 Thunder | Or Choose to deal 2d8 Acid, 2d8 Cold, 2d8 Fire, 2d8 Lightning, 2d8 Poison Creates a Surface on impact. | Range: 18 m (60 ft)
- How to get:
  - Sold by traders, found in containers, and carried by enemies
  - Above Gut's chambers in the rafters in the Shattered Sanctum

### Scroll of Feather Fall
- Type: Scroll | Rarity: Common | Act: Act One
- Effect: Grants Feather Fall Range: Self | AoE: 9 m (30 ft) Radius
- How to get:
  - Sold by traders, found in containers, and carried by enemies
  - In the Apothecary's Cellar at X: -666 Y: -356 on the table
  - In Baelen's Pack in the Underdark

### Scroll of Revivify
- Type: Scroll | Rarity: Common | Act: Act One
- Effect: Revives a Companion with 1 hit point. Range: 9 m (30 ft)
- How to get:
  - Starting item for most playable characters
  - Sold by some Traders
  - Among a pile of items in the Shattered Sanctum at X: 275 Y: -30

### Scroll of Summon Quasit
- Type: Scroll | Rarity: Common | Act: Act One
- How to get:
  - In the Apothecary's Cellar behind the moveable bookshelf, in the casket immediately to the right as entering the cavernous room at X: -666 Y: -332
