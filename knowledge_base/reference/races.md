# Baldur's Gate 3 - Races

There are 11 playable races in Baldur's Gate 3 . Many playable races are divided into subraces , which further defines a character's strengths, abilities, and appearance. Refer to individual Race pages for further details. Unless otherwise noted, spells gained from race may only be cast once per Long Rest. In D&D 5e, playable races tend to receive fixed ability score bonuses as racial bonuses. Baldur's Gate 3 instead awards each race the following ability score bonuses for the player to freely allocate: +2 to any Ability Score +1 to any other Ability Score

Unlike D&D 5e's fixed racial bonuses, BG3 gives every race a free ability score allocation: **+2 to any ability score and +1 to any other**. The per-race differences below are base speed, proficiencies and features (race-level applies to all its subraces).

Halflings and Gnomes are Small; all other playable races are Medium.

## Human

- Speed: Standard 9 m (30 ft)
- Proficiencies: Weapons Spears Pikes Halberds Glaives Armour Light armour Shields Skills One Skill of choice
- Features: Human Versatility Increased Carrying Capacity by 25%
